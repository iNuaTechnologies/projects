The problems

Where the helpline needs more support.

Four in Ten Calls Missed

40% of calls went unanswered in the first half of the year. Weak networks drop calls, and demand triples in an emergency.

Callers Wait Too Long

Calls take 30 to 60 seconds to answer. A child at risk may not have that long.

Follow-Ups Fall Through

Follow-ups depend on someone remembering. Reports show blanks, and there is no structured way to hear back from callers.
