The High-Investment / High-Return Paradox

Hybrid sunflower seed requires ~37,000 ETB/ha in input costs. While gross revenue potential is high (>130,000 ETB/ha), farmers fear adopting an unfamiliar crop, and banks fear loan defaults under rainfed farming conditions.

Deploying hundreds of field agronomists across diverse Woredas is financially impossible. Without scalable outreach, the 100,000 MT capacity remains under-utilized.
