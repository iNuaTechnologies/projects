Kenya School Solarization Strategy

Automated Data Collection & Project Implementation

Transitioning private primary schools in Kenya from firewood to clean, sustainable solar energy.

Finding schools and transitioning them to solar requires precise technical specs like kilowatt capacity, phase grids, and populations. Reaching and qualifying the right private schools, manually, across 47 counties is slow and causes coordination errors without a structured process.

Deploying territory marketing agents. Each agent surveys up to 5 schools per month, establishing relationships and feeding data directly to the dashboard.

A unified WhatsApp-based surveying pipeline that gathers data directly from school heads, feeding a real-time dashboard.
