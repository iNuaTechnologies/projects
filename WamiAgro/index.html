WamiAgro serves 70,000+ smallholder farmers across 8 regions of Ghana, from onboarding and savings to market access and farm monitoring

Farmers in Kintampo decide when to plant based on rainfall they cannot predict, and extension officers cannot reach every farmer in time.

Today's SMS and IVR are one-way. Many farmers use simple phones and may not read.

WamiAgro needs a natural, fluent Twi voice that farmers can call and be called by, on any phone, with a clear path to an extension officer.

The right advice, at the right stage of the season, through a simple phone call in Twi.

Pilot Structure

Scale

~30 Farmers

The Laangum group in the Kintampo area: a single, well-known cohort to test voice quality, adoption, and escalation with WamiAgro's field team close at hand.

Scope

Maize, in Twi

One crop and one language to isolate the agronomic calendar and refine the advice flow. Weather and market information included from day one.

Duration

2 Months

5,000 voice minutes of practical, stage-matched advice across the busiest part of the maize season, with a review at the end of month two.

Language

Fluent Twi Voice

Natural spoken Twi for the pilot. Additional group languages added from the list WamiAgro will share.

Channel

Inbound & Outbound

A hotline farmers can call, and proactive calls from Nuru and Chris for weather alerts and seasonal advice.

Process

Officer Referral

One agreed process for handing chemical and diagnosis questions to WamiAgro's extension officers.
